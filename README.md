# Adventure Systems

> V1.0

## 简体中文

整合 Curios 饰品、货币钱包与商店、FTB 任务交互和情境提示的冒险辅助模组。

### 核心功能

- **饰品**：心之钢提供战斗成长与生命强化；失乐园根据已品尝的不同食物提升伤害；悬浮背包提供飞行；钱包用于保存货币。
- **钱包与商店**：支持货币存取、兑换、掉落货币磁吸、余额 HUD，以及商品购买、材料出售、任务解锁条件和多种奖励形式。
- **FTB 任务联动**：将物品绑定到任务并快捷跳转；支持本地收藏、黑名单，以及符合条件的重复物品任务批量提交。
- **情境提示**：在加载和暂停界面显示多语言提示，可根据维度、群系、进度和携带物品等条件筛选。

### 使用

装备 Curios 钱包后按 **U** 打开钱包与商店；手持钱包右键切换货币磁吸。鼠标悬停在已绑定任务的物品上，按 **G** 打开关联任务，按 **Alt + G** 选择关联任务。按 **F6** 进入 KineticCore 配置中心，可管理饰品、商店、任务绑定和提示；服务器内容的编辑需要管理员权限。

**货币说明：** 默认货币使用 KubeJS 物品 ID；整合包须提供对应物品，或在配置中替换为实际存在的物品。

### 前置依赖

必需：Forge 47.4.2+、KineticCore 26.9.22+、Curios API 5.10.0+、FTB Library、FTB Quests、FTB Teams。可选：Refined Storage、Sophisticated Backpacks、JEI。

## English

Combines Curios accessories, a currency wallet and shop, FTB quest interactions, and contextual tips for adventure-focused modpacks.

### Features

- **Accessories:** Heart of Steel grants combat progression and extra health; Paradise Lost boosts damage based on distinct foods eaten; Levitation Backpack grants flight; Currency Wallet stores currencies.
- **Wallet and shop:** Currency deposits, withdrawals, exchanges, dropped-currency collection, a balance HUD, item purchases, material sales, quest prerequisites, and multiple reward types.
- **FTB quest integration:** Item-to-quest links and shortcuts, local favorites and blacklists, and bulk submission of eligible repeatable item tasks.
- **Contextual tips:** Multilingual loading and pause-screen tips filtered by dimension, biome, advancement, inventory, and other conditions.

### Usage

Equip the Curios wallet and press **U** to open the wallet and shop. Right-click while holding the wallet to toggle currency collection. Hover over an item linked to a quest and press **G** to open its linked quest or **Alt + G** to choose one. Press **F6** to open the KineticCore configuration center for accessories, shops, quest links, and tips. Editing server-managed content requires administrator permission.

**Currency setup:** Default currencies reference KubeJS item IDs. Provide those items in the modpack or replace their IDs with existing items in the configuration.

### Requirements

Required: Forge 47.4.2+, KineticCore 26.9.22+, Curios API 5.10.0+, FTB Library, FTB Quests, and FTB Teams. Optional: Refined Storage, Sophisticated Backpacks, and JEI.
